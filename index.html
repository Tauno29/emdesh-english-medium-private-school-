01 · Our school

Learning in English, growing in Okahao.

Em-Desh English Medium Private School is a private school in Okahao Town, in the Okahao Constituency of Namibia’s Omusati Region. Every lesson is taught in English.

We welcome learners from Kindergarten and Pre-Primary through Grade 7, supported by qualified teaching staff in settled, well-run classrooms.

The school offers on-site hostel (boarding) facilities, and learners take part in educational trips, national commemorations and other student activities throughout the year.

Prospective learners complete a placement assessment before formal admission, so each child begins at the right level.

We are situated at the ELCIN Centre in Okahao. Em-Desh is fully registered with the Ministry of Education, Innovation, Youth, Sports, Arts and Culture.

Our mission

We strive to provide quality education and lay a strong foundation for our learners’ future.

02 · Academics

From first steps to Grade 7.

Kindergarten, Pre-Primary and Grades 1 to 7, taught in English and supported by qualified teachers.

Kindergarten · Pre-Primary

A confident start

Early English, number sense and the routines of school life — a gentle, structured beginning for the youngest learners.

Grades 1–3

Strong foundations

Reading, writing and mathematics in English, built steadily alongside habits of curiosity, care and respect for others.

Grades 4–7

Growing independence

Deeper subject learning, independent study and the confidence to move on to secondary school.

  • English medium of learning
  • Oshindonga as another language
  • Basic Afrikaans
  • Jolly Phonics curriculum at Junior Primary
  • More media used in our learning
  • Compulsory spelling quizzes
  • Extra art lessons (visual & instruments)
  • Compulsory agriculture / gardening in practice, Grades 4–7
  • Qualified staff and good customer care
  • Good discipline among learners
  • New hostel accommodation with warm water
  • Excellent results
