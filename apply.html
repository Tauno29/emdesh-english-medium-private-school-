Hostel rules

These rules exist to ensure that the hostel functions harmoniously and effectively and to safeguard the interests and rights of all the boarders. They may by notice be amended from time to time and as hostel circumstances may demand. They are not exhaustive and any conduct not specifically mentioned herein, but which in the reasonable opinions of the hostel committee may be deemed inappropriate will also be deemed included herein.

  1. Discipline and punctuality must be given paramount importance.
  2. Learners are informed to behave in good manners with hostel staff and management.
  3. Learners are advised to give importance to health and hygiene. In case of any ill-health, it should be brought to the notice of the hostel warden immediately.
  4. The use of mobile phones, musical instruments and reading unwanted books are strictly prohibited.
  5. Other than parents/guardians, no one else is allowed to come to the hostel. Contact number for permitted person to visit the hostel is given above.
  6. No learner is allowed to go to the hostel without permission.
  7. No phone calls are permitted during school hours except emergency calls.
  8. All learners are expected to be good discipline at the hostel. Learners with bad/ ill-discipline can be dismissed from the hostel, parents must talk to their children all the time.
  9. Learners should carefully take care of furniture provided to them.
  10. In case of breaking the furniture, doors, windows and walls concerned learners and parents should bear the charges of repairing or replacement.
  11. Learners should use water and electricity wisely.
  12. When parents / guardians visit the hostel, they must meet with matrons and sign in visitor’s register.
  13. During the hostel admission the parent / guardians should give correct address, phone number to the chief matron and room matrons and school patron.
  14. All learners will report 11:00 - 16:30 from Home weekends or Holiday, and should be picked up before 15:00 to the weekend or holiday.
  15. On home weekend days learners are always to be released by 12:00 PM. No early pick up are allowed.
  16. Tea is 6:30 am, break time 10:00, Lunch time 13:00 pm and dinner at 19H00.
  17. NB: Hostel fee is N$ 1000.00 and must be paid every month from January – December.
  18. Due date of both school and Hostel fees is the 7th of every month and failing to pay per due date, a penalty of N$ 100.00 will be charged, accumulating on every outstanding month until all payments are settled.
  19. Hostel and School payment should be done at the bank and submit your proof of payment at school for recording purpose.

Please take note that

  1. Registration fee is non-refundable.
  2. During lockdown period, learners will still proceed with learning through distance or e-learning. Therefore, parents are expected to pay the full school and hostel fees during that period.
  3. Both School and hostel fees are payable for 12 months. Parents are expected to settle their child’s School fees and SDF before the child receive the school progress report each term.
  4. January fees are due on the 7th of January or Parents should settle the first payment before the child start attending classes from each holiday.
  5. All school contributions are compulsory to all learners e.g tea, tour, fun day, end term year party.
  6. No refunds on school and hostel fees.
  7. Every learner is expected to have full school uniform by February 2027. No child will be allowed to attend classes without full uniform from March and no other outfits/ modified uniforms are allowed at our school.
  8. Failure to pay school, hostel fees and school development fund you will be handed over to debt collectors.

NB: payment is always done a month in advance / ahead by due date 7th of every month. If parent failed to pay by the due date, penalty of N$ 100.00 will be charged accumulating every day from the due date to the day the payment will be settled.
